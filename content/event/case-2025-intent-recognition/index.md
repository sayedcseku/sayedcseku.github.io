Two papers accepted and presented by coauthors at IEEE CASE 2025 (Los Angeles) on maritime intent recognition:

1. **Early Classification of Intentions for Maritime Domains Using Deep Learning Models** — Feature-aware LSTM, Bi-LSTM, and Transformer classifiers for early vessel intent prediction.

2. **Proactive Maritime Threat Prediction: Vessel Intent Classification with LSTMs and Transformers Using a Sliding Window Approach** — Rolling-threat prediction with sliding-window formulation for real-time situational awareness.
