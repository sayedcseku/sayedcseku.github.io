I presented ThreatMap at the 26th International Conference on Harbor, Maritime and Multimodal Logistic Modeling & Simulation (University of La Laguna, Santa Cruz de Tenerife, Spain). The framework supports operator trust by providing intuitive, real-time visualization of evolving threats and the factors driving model-based predictions.
