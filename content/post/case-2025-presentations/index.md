Two papers accepted and presented by coauthors at **IEEE CASE 2025** in Los Angeles:

1. **Early Classification of Intentions for Maritime Domains Using Deep Learning Models** — Feature-aware LSTM, Bi-LSTM, and Transformer classifiers for early vessel behavior prediction.

2. **Proactive Maritime Threat Prediction: Vessel Intent Classification with LSTMs and Transformers Using a Sliding Window Approach** — Rolling-threat prediction with sliding windows for real-time situational awareness.
