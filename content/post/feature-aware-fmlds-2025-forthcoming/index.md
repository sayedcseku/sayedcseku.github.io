Our paper **"Feature-Aware Deep Learning for Maritime Intent Recognition"** has been accepted for the *IEEE International Conference on Future Machine Learning and Data Science (FMLDS 2025)*. The work achieves ~97% accuracy on seven maritime behaviors using only initial trajectory segments, with models incorporating bearing-rate, CPA/TCPA, and motion derivatives.
