I presented **ThreatMap: A Framework for Enhancing Security Awareness and Decision-Making for Naval Agents** at the **26th International Conference on Harbor, Maritime and Multimodal Logistic Modeling & Simulation (HMS 2024)** in Santa Cruz de Tenerife, Spain. The framework provides interpretable, real-time visualization of maritime threats for naval operators.
