I presented **NavySim: A Multi-Vessel Simulation and Analysis Engine for Naval Domains** at the **IEEE Conference on Games (CoG) 2024** in Milan, Italy. The work introduces a Unity-based simulator for maritime training and naval research, featuring real-time threat heatmaps and HMM-based intent recognition.
