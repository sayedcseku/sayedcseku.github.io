At the **Center for Digital Cardiovascular Innovations**, University of Miami Miller School of Medicine / UHealth System (directed by **Dr. Yiannis S. Chatzizisis**, Chief, Division of Cardiovascular Medicine), this research develops deep learning and computational modeling to assist interventional cardiologists in safety-critical decisions before and during coronary interventions.

## Core Research Thrusts

- **Multi-Class Intravascular Image Segmentation:** Neural network models for multi-class segmentation of lumen, vessel wall, and plaque composition (calcium, fibrous, fibrolipidic) across High-Definition Intravascular Ultrasound (HD-IVUS) and NIRS-IVUS pullbacks. Trained on a curated corpus of 40,000+ expert-labeled IVUS frames and benchmarked against expert inter-observer variability.
- **Calcium Scoring for Stent Outcome Prediction:** Automated IVUS-based calcium morphology quantification to predict stent under-expansion prior to intervention, enabling proactive procedural planning.
- **Simulation-Based Clinical Decision Support for PCI:** Strategy recommendation for calcified lesions (non-compliant balloon, intravascular lithotripsy, atherectomy) and bifurcation stenting techniques learned from a virtual simulation corpus of 1,200 lesion phenotypes and patient-specific coronary anatomies.
